Deeply nested collections (kernel selftest TestCollectionOverflow)

VID:PID    xxxx:xxxx (none, not a real device)
Type       Synthetic test descriptor (not a real device)
Source     https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/tools/testing/selftests/hid/tests/test_hid_core.py

Notes
  - Many nested collections inside a mouse application collection. A parser must not crash or
    overflow its collection stack.
  - A parser that loses track of the nesting can end up with a mouse whose report size is 0.
