Source     https://github.com/hathach/tinyusb/blob/master/src/class/hid/hid_device.h

Notes
  - Transcribed from the TinyUSB macros, as in its hid_boot_interface example. Two interfaces, no
    report IDs: the standard keyboard + mouse layout of most simple devices.

  Interface 0: class 03 (HID), subclass 01 (boot), protocol 01 (keyboard), endpoint max packet 8 bytes
